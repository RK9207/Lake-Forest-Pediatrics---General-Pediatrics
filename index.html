Care for each stage of growing up

From bringing a newborn home to getting a teenager ready for sports, you can ask questions and plan routine care with one pediatric practice.

Well-child visits and immunizations

Checkups give you time to talk about growth, development, sleep, or changes you’ve noticed. Your care team can review which vaccines are due and help you plan the next steps in preventive care.

Newborn care and lactation support

Bring questions about feeding, weight, and early routines to your baby’s first visits. Lactation support is available for families who want help with breastfeeding.

School, sports, and camp physicals

Ask which exam your child needs and bring any required forms. If a team or school has a deadline, call early to discuss scheduling.

Care when your child is sick

Call for sick-visit options. The team can advise you on how to be seen, and on-site testing may help the clinician evaluate symptoms when appropriate.

Meet the people behind your child’s care

Lake Forest Pediatrics brings together board-certified pediatricians and nurse practitioners to care for children and young adults through age 22. During a checkup, you can talk through what’s changing now and what to watch for as your child grows.

Physicians are on staff at Northwestern Lake Forest Hospital. The practice has served Lake County families for more than 50 years.

Planning your first visit

Find out how to check availability, ask about insurance, and reach the office that works for you.

Are you accepting new patients?

Call to check new-patient availability at your preferred office. Let the team know your child’s age and whether you’re looking for a checkup or another type of visit.

What ages do you see?

The practice cares for children from birth through age 22, including teenagers and college-age young adults.

How do I schedule a visit?

Call the practice or request an appointment. The team can help confirm availability and explain what to bring. Established patients can also use the patient portal for eligible visits.

How do I confirm my insurance coverage?

Check with your insurer to confirm coverage for the office and visit type. If you need practice or billing information for that check, call with your plan details before the visit.

What if my child gets sick?

Call the practice for sick-visit options. Medical professionals are on call after hours. For a medical emergency, call 911.

What should I do before my baby’s first visit?

Read the practice’s new-patient information before delivery. After discharge, call to arrange your baby’s first visit and ask what you should bring.
