**\#\# IMG-HERO-01 \- Hero**

**\#\#\# Placeholder purpose**

Help a caregiver picture a calm, everyday pediatric visit. The image should suggest that the child can participate and the caregiver can ask questions. The headline, office selector, and call button carry the service, location, and next step.

**\#\#\# Recommended source type**

AI-generated lifestyle/editorial image appropriate

**\#\#\# Recommended composition**

\- \*\*Orientation and subject placement\*\* — Use 4:3 landscape. Keep the main exchange near the center, with the people facing inward or slightly toward the page copy on the left.  
\- \*\*Negative space and crop\*\* — Leave breathing room around faces, shoulders, and any meaningful gesture. Use a quiet background. The image sits beside the copy, so it needs no space for overlaid text.  
\- \*\*Desktop and mobile\*\* — The image is in the right column on desktop and below the contact actions and office links on mobile. Keep the key interaction legible at a narrow width. Check center cropping with the slot’s \`object-fit: cover\`.

**\#\#\# Option 1 \- A well-child conversation**

Create a photorealistic 4:3 landscape editorial photograph of a routine well-child visit. At eye level, a casually dressed pediatric clinician listens to a child around 6–8 years old while the caregiver sits beside the child. The child is mid-sentence and starting a small, natural gesture. The caregiver is relaxed and attentive. Use an ordinary community pediatric exam room with indirect daylight, warm white walls, light wood, and a hint of muted green. Frame the three people in a close, slightly off-center triangle within the middle of the image. Use a 50 mm documentary feel, natural skin texture, and enough depth of field to keep all three expressions readable. Leave quiet margins for the mobile crop. Show everyday clothes, plausible posture and hands, and no visible charts, text, logos, or medical procedures.

**\#\#\# Option 2 \- A calm arrival**

Create a photorealistic 4:3 landscape editorial photograph of a caregiver and a child around 4–6 entering an unbranded pediatric office for a planned visit. Photograph them from the side at the moment the child pauses and looks ahead while the caregiver slows down to match their pace. Let a bright threshold and soft shapes of a small waiting area imply the destination. Use a 35 mm environmental composition, gentle morning light, natural denim and knit layers, warm cream and pale blue, and moderate depth of field. Place both figures close together near the center with room around their heads and shoulders. Keep the scene ordinary and quietly welcoming. Avoid posed smiles, signage, screens, branded objects, and recognizable office architecture.

**\#\#\# Option 3 \- Space for a teenager’s questions**

Create a photorealistic 4:3 landscape healthcare editorial photograph of a teen around 14–16 and a clinician speaking during a routine visit. The teen is the main participant, seated upright in a casual sweatshirt and speaking while the clinician listens with an open posture. A caregiver sits a little back, still clearly present. Use a seated eye-level, over-the-shoulder angle with a 50 mm lens feel. Keep the teen’s expression and the clinician’s attentive profile within the central crop, with the caregiver secondary. Use warm window light and a subdued cream-and-blue palette in a simple unbranded consult room. Avoid visible text, patient information, examination equipment, forced smiles, and over-retouched skin.

**\#\#\# Recommended option**

\*\*Option 1 — A well-child conversation.\*\* It shows the routine care and two-way communication described in the hero. The close grouping keeps the interaction readable on mobile while leaving the call action visually dominant.

**\#\#\# Real-image note**

A practice-approved visit photo with permission from everyone depicted would also fit. Use any client-hosted stock only after confirming reuse rights. An AI image should illustrate a generic pediatric visit, with no suggestion that it depicts a Lake Forest Pediatrics clinician, patient, or office.

**\#\# IMG-PROVIDER-01 \- Care team**

\#\#\# Placeholder purpose

Put a recognizable face to the care-team introduction and the nearby provider directory link. The copy establishes the practice’s qualifications. An authentic photo can help families recognize people they may meet.

\#\#\# Recommended source type

Real provider/team image preferred

\#\#\# Recommended composition

\- \*\*Orientation and subject placement\*\* — Use 3:2 landscape. Favor a compact grouping of two or three people so faces remain recognizable. Keep the main subjects within the middle two-thirds.  
\- \*\*Negative space and crop\*\* — Leave modest headroom and space outside the group. Use a quiet, softly focused setting. Keep any names or role information in the HTML caption.  
\- \*\*Desktop and mobile\*\* — On desktop the image sits to the right of the care-team copy. On mobile it follows that copy and the provider directory link. Keep faces readable when centered at a narrow width, and avoid tight crops through faces or meaningful gestures.

The AI options below are visual references for selecting or commissioning authentic photography.

\#\#\# Option 1 \- A small team portrait

Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape editorial reference for a portrait of a fictional pediatric clinician and nurse practitioner. Two adults in their 30s–60s stand comfortably beside one another near a softly lit window, with relaxed shoulders and natural, slight smiles. Dress them in business-casual clinic clothing and neutral scrubs without badges or logos. Use a waist-up frame, 55 mm lens feel, and soft daylight in a generic outpatient setting. Keep both faces in focus within the middle two-thirds, with room around their heads and shoulders. Use a warm cream and muted green palette, natural skin texture, and realistic proportions. Avoid rigid posing, visible text, and any resemblance to identifiable clinicians.

\#\#\# Option 2 \- A conversation between colleagues

Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape documentary reference of two fictional pediatric clinicians in their 30s–60s having a brief conversation between visits. Photograph them in three-quarter profile at a quiet counter, one listening while the other makes a small, open-hand gesture. Use a 40 mm environmental lens feel, a candid waist-up crop, and soft side light in a modest, unbranded clinic. Keep both faces and the gesture close to center, with understated wood and pale blue in the background. Use ordinary clinic apparel and believable posture. Exclude patients, visible records, screens, logos, text, and exaggerated expressions.

\#\#\# Option 3 \- Preparing the room

Create a photorealistic 3:2 landscape editorial reference from a generic pediatric exam room. At the center, show only the hands and sleeves of a fictional clinician placing a clean pediatric stethoscope on a small work surface before a visit. Use a close 60 mm lens feel, natural window light, warm white and soft sage, and enough depth of field to make the simple action and the room context recognizable. Leave space on each side so the hand and instrument remain intact on mobile. Use anatomically plausible hands and realistic equipment. Exclude faces, badges, text, charts, needles, and staged props.

\#\#\# Recommended option

\*\*Option 1 — A small team portrait, recreated with the actual care team.\*\* A straightforward portrait lets visitors recognize the people introduced in the copy. The close grouping holds up at phone width. The other options are useful references for collaborative or environmental photography, but offer less help recognizing the team.
